IUBAT Bus Tracker

Privacy

How the bus tracker uses information.

Viewing buses

Anyone can see the buses without an account. We do not track who is looking.

Contributor account (optional, no password)

  • To share a bus location you pick a username, an emoji avatar, optionally your route, admission batch and a small photo. These are public on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to.
  • Instead of a password, your phone keeps a secret recovery code. We store only a one-way hash of it. Anyone with the code can use your account, so keep it private.
  • Your profile is also cached on your phone so the app opens instantly.
  • We keep how far you shared on each trip (to count XP, badges and weekly champions), never where you were.
  • Photos are cut to a small square. Riders can report a photo; the transport office reviews every report and can remove photos, reset rude usernames and turn off uploads or sharing for an account.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time from your profile.

Bus locations

Each bus phone sends the bus's location while it is on duty. We keep only the latest position of each bus, not a history.

If you share your location for a bus

  • Sharing is optional and starts only when you tap Share location and agree. You can stop at any time. It is only possible during trip times (mornings until the bus reaches campus, evenings until it reaches the end of its route).
  • Your location is checked automatically (are you on that bus route and moving with the bus?). Only a checked location is used to move the bus on the map.
  • Other riders see the bus, not you.
  • The transport office can see which username is sharing, to prevent misuse, and can block sharing.
  • We keep only your last few minutes of positions while you share, and delete them when you stop or the bus reaches the end of the route.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, we store your browser's push address (from Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft), your route and stop, and which notification types you want. Messages are kept for up to 3 days. Turning notifications off deletes the subscription.

Feedback

Feedback goes only to the transport office, with the route you choose, your username if you have one, and a contact only if you give it.

Sign-in protection

To stop abuse we briefly keep counters of requests per network address (for example new accounts per hour), for up to 24 hours.

Map

The map uses OpenStreetMap data served by OpenFreeMap. Your browser loads map images from them, which means they can see your network address, as with any website.

Questions

Contact the IUBAT transport office.
